# Junior business analyst
## Professional Profile
Junior Business Analyst with an experience in business process management and strategic development across various industries and markets. I specialize in analyzing business needs, eliciting and documenting requirements, and identifying opportunities for process improvement. I combine practical business knowledge with business analysis skills to translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements and effective solutions.

* **Requirements:** Elicitation, User Stories (INVEST), Use Cases, and Change Management.
* **Technical:** SQL, API (Postman), Git, and SDLC (Agile/Scrum/Kanban).
* **Modeling:** Visualizing processes via UML, BPMN, and ER diagrams.
* **Tools:** Expert user of Jira and Confluence for backlog management.
* **Strategy:** Stakeholder mapping, MVP definition, and Product Roadmapping.
* **Design:** Prototyping and UX fundamentals.
